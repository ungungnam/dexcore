## Final diagnosis

**Case C as pre-registered (the steps of z do not follow the steps of the contact) is rejected: they do follow them. Whether the lower persistence of z than of the dense map contributed to the Stage-2 failure was not tested. z is locally predictable in the R² sense when its current value is known (R² <<p_taco_8_probe_r2:.2f>> / <<p_arctic_8_probe_r2:.2f>> at h = 8), but most of that is persistence. What a learned model adds to persistence is clear for pairs that start at the first frame of a window on both datasets; for pairs that start eight or more frames in, it is marginal at h = 1 and 4 and clear at h = 8 on TACO (<<sp_taco_8_probe_t8_gain_rmse:.1%>>), while on ARCTIC the probe is worse than persistence at h = 1 (<<sp_arctic_1_probe_t8_gain_rmse:.1%>>) and indistinguishable from it at h = 4. Started from the true initial latent, a predictor is no longer clearly better than Stage 2 after eight frames. These measurements do not decide whether Stage 2 failed because of the open-loop formulation (H1) or because z has little learnable dynamics beyond persistence (the first criterion of H2): the pre-registered rule returns Case A on TACO (borderline) and Case B on ARCTIC, and neither a closed-loop rollout nor a horizon beyond eight frames was measured.**
