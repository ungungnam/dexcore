**What is tested.** No training. For every pair of GT frames (t, t + h) the dense step ΔC = ‖C_t − C_{t+h}‖ and the latent step Δz = ‖z_t − z_{t+h}‖ (standardised z) are computed and each is divided by its mean over 200 000 random pairs of *training* frames, so that 1.0 means "as far apart as two unrelated frames". The Stage-1 teacher step ΔT (exact R2 + wrench distance) is carried along as a secondary quantity, because z was trained to organise frames by that distance.
