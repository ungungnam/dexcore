For h = 4 the figures show one example of each *kind* of low-ΔC / high-Δz transition — the member of its kind with the median latent step, so the examples are typical rather than extreme — and one typical (median) transition of each well-behaved quadrant, with the two contact maps, their difference, the R2 change (participation sets, total amount), the teacher step and ‖Δz‖ (`figures/fig5_selection_<ds>.csv`; the counts per kind are printed in the row labels).

* **Weak-contact start.** The starting frame has little contact (norm below the training 10 % quantile); a few weakly touching points appear or disappear, which is a small L2 change and a large relative one.
* **Structural step.** The contact norm is normal but the transition carries a participation flip or a large teacher (R2 + wrench) step — on TACO, a finger joining a firm multi-finger grasp. The dense map barely changes, the structure does, and z follows the structure it was trained to organise.
* **Neither.** Not a weak-contact start, unchanged participation set, teacher step below its "high" threshold: these are the transitions that actually match "small contact change, large latent change".
