**Q6. Is there enough evidence to continue developing z as the temporal state?** Yes, as far as the representation is concerned: it decodes well (Stage 2), it is locally predictable from its current value, and its steps follow the contact's. What the evidence does not settle is whether a different way of predicting z would recover what Stage 2 lost: a state-conditioned prediction is easy only while the state is true and recent, and no measurement here covers a model that must carry its own predicted state over a long horizon. That question needs a closed-loop test, which is outside this diagnostic.
